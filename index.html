Wristory

Know your rotation.

An iPhone app for tracking which watch you wear, how often, and how well it keeps time.

Log what you wear

Pick the watch on your wrist and tap once. Over time you build a picture of what you actually reach for, rather than what you think you reach for.

See your rotation

A breakdown of where your wrist time goes, by month, quarter, year or all time. The watch you were sure was your favourite often is not.

Check accuracy

Rest a mechanical watch against the phone and Wristory listens to it tick, then works out how many seconds a day it gains or loses, along with beat rate and beat error. It is an estimate from a phone microphone rather than a substitute for a watchmaker's timegrapher, but it is good for spotting changes over time.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you log leaves the device. You can export the whole thing to a spreadsheet whenever you like. See the privacy policy for the detail.
