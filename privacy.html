WristCheck

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

WristCheck is made to keep your information on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you put into the app is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app stores

  • The watches in your collection: names, colors, icon style and any photos you add.
  • Your wear log: which watch you wore on which day.
  • Accuracy readings: the measured rate, beat rate, beat error and position, with the time each was taken.
  • Your settings, such as clock style and sort order.

All of this is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. The developer can't see it.

Microphone

The Accuracy tab uses the microphone only while you are taking a reading, after you start it and allow access. The sound is analyzed on your device as it comes in. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere; only the resulting numbers are kept. The microphone turns off when the reading ends, when you leave the tab, or when the app goes into the background.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo of a watch, you choose it from your library or take it with the camera, and iOS may ask for permission first. Only the photo you pick is used. It is resized and stored on your device.

Internet

The app goes online for one thing: getting the exact time from a public time service (time.now) so the clock on the Today tab is accurate. That request contains no information about you or your watches. As with any internet request, the time service can see your device's IP address. If it can't be reached, the app uses your phone's clock.

Exporting and importing

Export creates a spreadsheet of your data and opens the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there, such as Files, Mail or AirDrop, is up to you. Import only reads the file you pick.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, your WristCheck data is included in them, under Apple's privacy terms.

Deleting your data

You can delete log entries, readings and watches inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

WristCheck does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the WristCheck website with a new effective date.
